# Aseuro Ecommerce Website — Project Brief

## Brand

- **Store name:** Aseuro
- **Domain:** aseurollc.com
- **Brand direction:** Premium and clean

## Products and Customers

- **Product categories:** Beauty products, skincare products, pet supplies, and oral-care products
- **Target customers:** A broad audience including young women, men, pet owners, and families
- **Launch catalogue:** 5–10 products
- **Price range:** $15–$30
- **Product images:** Real product photos are available

## Design

- **Colour direction:** White, soft beige, and warm brown
- **Typography:** Modern and simple
- **Reference direction:** Minimal, premium luxury-store aesthetic; no specific website reference supplied

## Required Pages

- Home
- Products
- Cart
- About Us
- Contact Us

## Orders and Customer Contact

- **Payments:** Credit and debit cards
- **Customer contact:** WhatsApp

## Required Features

- Offers
- Customer reviews
- FAQ

Card payments require a checkout experience; include Checkout as part of the ordering flow.
